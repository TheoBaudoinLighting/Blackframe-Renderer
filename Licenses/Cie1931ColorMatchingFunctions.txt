CIE 1931 colour-matching functions, 2 degree observer

The source CSV was adapted to C++ initializers in
Blackframe/Renderer/Source/Cie1931ColorMatchingFunctions.inc. The explicit
wavelength column was replaced by the row index, where index zero represents
360 nm and each following index advances by 1 nm. The 471 XYZ numeric triplets
are otherwise unmodified.

CIE 2019, Colour-matching functions of CIE 1931 standard colorimetric observer,
International Commission on Illumination (CIE), Vienna, AT.
